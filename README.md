#Supply Chain Performance & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

## Introduction
This project delivers an interactive, data-driven analytical solution designed to track, monitor, and optimize end-to-end supply chain metrics and inventory performance across 100 product SKUs. It bridges the gap between raw operational data and strategic decision-making.

## The Problem
Modern supply chains often suffer from visibility bottlenecks, inefficient inventory management, and a lack of real-time tracking across key performance indicators (KPIs) like stock levels, fulfillment rates, and lead times. Without a centralized analytical view, organizations face risks such as:
* Stockouts and overstock scenarios.
* Delayed response times to demand fluctuations.
* Inability to quickly evaluate SKU-level performance and supplier efficiency.


## The Solution
This project implements an interactive Power BI dashboard that transforms complex supply chain datasets into actionable insights. 
* **Comprehensive SKU Tracking:** Monitors 100 product SKUs across critical operational dimensions.
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Allows stakeholders to slice and dice data by product categories, regions, and timeframes.
* **Proactive Monitoring:** Highlights low-stock alerts, turnover ratios, and fulfillment bottlenecks to drive operational excellence and continuous improvement (Lean/Six Sigma principles).


## Tools Used
* **Power BI:** For data modeling, DAX measures formulation, and interactive data visualization.
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** For initial data cleaning, structuring, and staging.
